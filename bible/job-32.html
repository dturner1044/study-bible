Gill on this chapter

Elihu Rebukes Job’s Friends

1So these three men ceased to1 answer Job, because he was righteous in his own eyes. 2Then was kindled the wrath of Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite, of the kindred of Ram: against Job was his wrath kindled, because he justified himself2 rather than God. 3Also against his three friends was his wrath kindled, because they had found no answer, and yet had condemned Job. 4Now Elihu had waited3 till Job had spoken, because they were elder4 than he. 5When Elihu saw that there was no answer in the mouth of these three men, then his wrath was kindled.

6And Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite answered and said, I am young5, and ye are very old; wherefore I was afraid, and durst6 not shew you mine opinion. 7I said, Days should speak, and multitude of years should teach wisdom. 8But there is a spirit in man: and the inspiration of the Almighty giveth them understanding. 9Great men are not always wise: neither do the aged understand judgment. 10Therefore I said, Hearken to me; I also will shew mine opinion. 11Behold, I waited for your words; I gave ear to your reasons7, whilst ye searched out what8 to say. 12Yea, I attended unto you, and, behold, there was none of you that convinced Job, or that answered his words: 13Lest ye should say, We have found out wisdom: God thrusteth him down, not man. 14Now he hath not directed9 his words against me: neither will I answer him with your speeches.

15They were amazed, they answered no more: they left10 off speaking. 16When I had waited, (for they spake not, but stood still, and answered no more;) 17I said, I will answer also my part, I also will shew mine opinion. 18For I am full of matter11, the spirit12 within me constraineth me. 19Behold, my belly is as wine which hath13 no vent; it is ready to burst like new bottles. 20I will speak, that I may be14 refreshed: I will open my lips and answer. 21Let me not, I pray you, accept any man’s person, neither let me give flattering titles unto man. 22For I know not to give flattering titles; in so doing my maker would soon take me away.

Notes

1 32:1 to…: Heb. from answering

2 32:2 himself: Heb. his soul

3 32:4 waited…: Heb. expected Job in words

4 32:4 elder: Heb. elder for days

5 32:6 young: Heb. few of days

6 32:6 durst…: Heb. feared

7 32:11 reasons: Heb. understandings

8 32:11 what…: Heb. words

9 32:14 directed: or, ordered

10 32:15 left…: Heb. removed speeches from themselves

11 32:18 matter: Heb. words

12 32:18 spirit…: Heb. spirit of my belly
