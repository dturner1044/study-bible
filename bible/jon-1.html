Gill on this chapter

Jonah Flees from the LORD

(Nahum 1:1–15)

1Now the word of the LORD came unto Jonah1 the son of Amittai, saying, 2Arise, go to Nineveh, that great city, and cry against it; for their wickedness is come up before me. 3But Jonah rose up to flee unto Tarshish from the presence of the LORD, and went down to Joppa; and he found a ship going to Tarshish: so he paid the fare thereof, and went down into it, to go with them unto Tarshish from the presence of the LORD.

The Great Storm

(Acts 27:13–26)

4But the LORD sent out2 a great wind into the sea, and there was a mighty tempest in the sea, so that the ship was like3 to be broken. 5Then the mariners were afraid, and cried every man unto his god, and cast forth the wares that were in the ship into the sea, to lighten it of them. But Jonah was gone down into the sides of the ship; and he lay, and was fast asleep. 6So the shipmaster came to him, and said unto him, What meanest thou, O sleeper? arise, call upon thy God, if so be that God will think upon us, that we perish not. 7And they said every one to his fellow, Come, and let us cast lots, that we may know for whose cause this evil is upon us. So they cast lots, and the lot fell upon Jonah. 8Then said they unto him, Tell us, we pray thee, for whose cause this evil is upon us; What is thine occupation? and whence comest thou? what is thy country? and of what people art thou? 9And he said unto them, I am an Hebrew; and I fear the LORD4, the God of heaven, which hath made the sea and the dry land. 10Then were the men exceedingly5 afraid, and said unto him, Why hast thou done this? For the men knew that he fled from the presence of the LORD, because he had told them.

Jonah Cast into the Sea

11Then said they unto him, What shall we do unto thee, that the sea may6 be calm unto us? for the sea wrought7, and was tempestuous. 12And he said unto them, Take me up, and cast me forth into the sea; so shall the sea be calm unto you: for I know that for my sake this great tempest is upon you. 13Nevertheless the men rowed8 hard to bring it to the land; but they could not: for the sea wrought9, and was tempestuous against them. 14Wherefore they cried unto the LORD, and said, We beseech thee, O LORD, we beseech thee, let us not perish for this man’s life, and lay not upon us innocent blood: for thou, O LORD, hast done as it pleased thee. 15So they took up Jonah, and cast him forth into the sea: and the sea ceased10 from her raging. 16Then the men feared the LORD exceedingly, and offered11 a sacrifice unto the LORD, and made vows.

17Now the LORD had prepared a great fish to swallow up Jonah. And Jonah was in the belly12 of the fish three days and three nights.

Notes

1 1:1 Jonah: Gr. Jonas

2 1:4 sent out: Heb. cast forth

3 1:4 was like…: Heb. thought to be broken

4 1:9 the LORD: or, JEHOVAH

5 1:10 exceedingly…: Heb. with great fear

6 1:11 may…: Heb. may be silent from us

7 1:11 wrought…: or, grew more and more tempestuous: Heb. went

8 1:13 rowed: Heb. digged

9 1:13 wrought…: or, grew more and more tempestuous: Heb. went

10 1:15 ceased: Heb. stood

11 1:16 offered…: Heb. sacrifice unto the LORD, and vowed vows

12 1:17 belly: Heb. bowels
