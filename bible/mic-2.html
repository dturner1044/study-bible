Gill on this chapter

Woe to Oppressors

1Woe to them that devise iniquity, and work evil upon their beds! when the morning is light, they practise it, because it is in the power of their hand. 2And they covet fields, and take them by violence; and houses, and take them away: so they oppress1 a man and his house, even a man and his heritage. 3Therefore thus saith the LORD; Behold, against this family do I devise an evil, from which ye shall not remove your necks; neither shall ye go haughtily: for this time is evil.

4In that day shall one take up a parable against you, and lament with a doleful2 lamentation, and say, We be utterly spoiled: he hath changed the portion of my people: how hath he removed it from me! turning3 away he hath divided our fields. 5Therefore thou shalt have none that shall cast a cord by lot in the congregation of the LORD.

Reproof of False Prophets

(Ezekiel 13:1–16)

6Prophesy ye4 not, say they to them that prophesy: they shall not prophesy to them, that they shall not take shame.

7O thou that art named the house of Jacob, is the spirit of the LORD straitened5? are these his doings? do not my words do good to him that walketh uprightly6? 8Even of late7 my people is risen up as an enemy: ye pull off the robe with the8 garment from them that pass by securely as men averse from war. 9The women9 of my people have ye cast out from their pleasant houses; from their children have ye taken away my glory for ever. 10Arise ye, and depart; for this is not your rest: because it is polluted, it shall destroy you, even with a sore destruction. 11If a man walking10 in the spirit and falsehood do lie, saying, I will prophesy unto thee of wine and of strong drink; he shall even be the prophet of this people.

The Remnant of Israel

(Micah 5:7–15)

12I will surely assemble, O Jacob, all of thee; I will surely gather the remnant of Israel; I will put them together as the sheep of Bozrah, as the flock in the midst of their fold: they shall make great noise by reason of the multitude of men. 13The breaker is come up before them: they have broken up, and have passed through the gate, and are gone out by it: and their king shall pass before them, and the LORD on the head of them.

Notes

1 2:2 oppress: or, defraud

2 2:4 a doleful…: Heb. a lamentation of lamentations

3 2:4 turning…: or, instead of restoring

4 2:6 Prophesy ye…: or, Prophesy not as they prophesy: Heb. Drop, etc

5 2:7 straitened: or, shortened?

6 2:7 uprightly: Heb. upright?

7 2:8 of late: Heb. yesterday

8 2:8 with the: Heb. over against a

9 2:9 women: or, wives

10 2:11 walking…: or, walk with the wind, and lie falsely
