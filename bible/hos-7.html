Gill on this chapter

Ephraim’s Iniquity

1When I would have healed Israel, then the iniquity of Ephraim was discovered, and the wickedness1 of Samaria: for they commit falsehood; and the thief cometh in, and the troop of robbers spoileth2 without. 2And they consider3 not in their hearts that I remember all their wickedness: now their own doings have beset them about; they are before my face. 3They make the king glad with their wickedness, and the princes with their lies. 4They are all adulterers, as an oven heated by the baker, who4 ceaseth from raising5 after he hath kneaded the dough, until it be leavened. 5In the day of our king the princes have made him sick with bottles6 of wine; he stretched out his hand with scorners. 6For they have made7 ready their heart like an oven, whiles they lie in wait: their baker sleepeth all the night; in the morning it burneth as a flaming fire. 7They are all hot as an oven, and have devoured their judges; all their kings are fallen: there is none among them that calleth unto me.

8Ephraim, he hath mixed himself among the people; Ephraim is a cake not turned. 9Strangers have devoured his strength, and he knoweth it not: yea, gray hairs are here8 and there upon him, yet he knoweth not. 10And the pride of Israel testifieth to his face: and they do not return to the LORD their God, nor seek him for all this.

11Ephraim also is like a silly dove without heart: they call to Egypt, they go to Assyria. 12When they shall go, I will spread my net upon them; I will bring them down as the fowls of the heaven; I will chastise them, as their congregation hath heard. 13Woe unto them! for they have fled from me: destruction9 unto them! because they have transgressed against me: though I have redeemed them, yet they have spoken lies against me. 14And they have not cried unto me with their heart, when they howled upon their beds: they assemble themselves for corn and wine, and they rebel against me. 15Though I have10 bound and strengthened their arms, yet do they imagine mischief against me. 16They return, but not to the most High: they are like a deceitful bow: their princes shall fall by the sword for the rage of their tongue: this shall be their derision in the land of Egypt.

Notes

1 7:1 wickedness: Heb. evils

2 7:1 spoileth: Heb. strippeth

3 7:2 consider…: Heb. say not to

4 7:4 who…: or, the raiser will cease

5 7:4 raising: or, waking

6 7:5 bottles…: or, heat through wine

7 7:6 made…: or, applied

8 7:9 here…: Heb. sprinkled

9 7:13 destruction: Heb. spoil

10 7:15 have…: or, chastened
