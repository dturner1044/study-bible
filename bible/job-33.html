Gill on this chapter

Elihu Rebukes Job

1Wherefore, Job, I pray thee, hear my speeches, and hearken to all my words. 2Behold, now I have opened my mouth, my tongue hath spoken in my mouth1. 3My words shall be of the uprightness of my heart: and my lips shall utter knowledge clearly. 4The Spirit of God hath made me, and the breath of the Almighty hath given me life. 5If thou canst answer me, set thy words in order before me, stand up. 6Behold, I am according to thy wish2 in God’s stead: I also am formed3 out of the clay. 7Behold, my terror shall not make thee afraid, neither shall my hand be heavy upon thee.

8Surely thou hast spoken in mine hearing4, and I have heard the voice of thy words, saying, 9I am clean without transgression, I am innocent; neither is there iniquity in me. 10Behold, he findeth occasions against me, he counteth me for his enemy, 11He putteth my feet in the stocks, he marketh all my paths. 12Behold, in this thou art not just: I will answer thee, that God is greater than man. 13Why dost thou strive against him? for he giveth5 not account of any of his matters.

14For God speaketh once, yea twice, yet man perceiveth it not. 15In a dream, in a vision of the night, when deep sleep falleth upon men, in slumberings upon the bed; 16Then he6 openeth the ears of men, and sealeth their instruction, 17That he may withdraw man from his purpose7, and hide pride from man. 18He keepeth back his soul from the pit, and his life from perishing8 by the sword.

19He is chastened also with pain upon his bed, and the multitude of his bones with strong pain: 20So that his life abhorreth bread, and his soul dainty9 meat. 21His flesh is consumed away, that it cannot be seen; and his bones that were not seen stick out. 22Yea, his soul draweth near unto the grave, and his life to the destroyers. 23If there be a messenger with him, an interpreter, one among a thousand, to shew unto man his uprightness: 24Then he is gracious unto him, and saith, Deliver him from going down to the pit: I have found a ransom10. 25His flesh shall be fresher than a child’s11: he shall return to the days of his youth: 26He shall pray unto God, and he will be favourable unto him: and he shall see his face with joy: for he will render unto man his righteousness. 27He12 looketh upon men, and if any say, I have sinned, and perverted that which was right, and it profited me not; 28He13 will deliver his soul from going into the pit, and his life shall see the light.

29Lo, all these things worketh God oftentimes14 with man, 30To bring back his soul from the pit, to be enlightened with the light of the living. 31Mark well, O Job, hearken unto me: hold thy peace, and I will speak. 32If thou hast any thing to say, answer me: speak, for I desire to justify thee. 33If not, hearken unto me: hold thy peace, and I shall teach thee wisdom.

Notes

1 33:2 in my mouth: Heb. in my palate

2 33:6 wish: Heb. mouth

3 33:6 formed: Heb. cut

4 33:8 hearing: Heb. ears

5 33:13 he giveth…: Heb. he answereth not

6 33:16 he…: Heb. he revealeth, or, uncovereth

7 33:17 purpose: Heb. work

8 33:18 from perishing: Heb. from passing

9 33:20 dainty…: Heb. meat of desire

10 33:24 a ransom: or, an atonement

11 33:25 a child’s: Heb. childhood

12 33:27 He…: or, He shall look upon men, and say

13 33:28 He…: or, He hath delivered my soul, etc, and my life

14 33:29 oftentimes: Heb. twice and thrice
