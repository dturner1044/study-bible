Gill on this chapter

A Call to Repentance

(Jeremiah 3:11–25; Zechariah 1:1–6)

1O Israel, return unto the LORD thy God; for thou hast fallen by thine iniquity. 2Take with you words, and turn to the LORD: say unto him, Take away all iniquity, and receive1 us graciously: so will we render the calves of our lips. 3Asshur shall not save us; we will not ride upon horses: neither will we say any more to the work of our hands, Ye are our gods: for in thee the fatherless findeth mercy.

A Promise of God’s Blessing

4I will heal their backsliding, I will love them freely: for mine anger is turned away from him. 5I will be as the dew unto Israel: he shall grow2 as the lily, and cast3 forth his roots as Lebanon. 6His branches shall spread4, and his beauty shall be as the olive tree, and his smell as Lebanon. 7They that dwell under his shadow shall return; they shall revive as the corn, and grow5 as the vine: the scent6 thereof shall be as the wine of Lebanon.

8Ephraim shall say, What have I to do any more with idols? I have heard him, and observed him: I am like a green fir tree. From me is thy fruit found. 9Who is wise, and he shall understand these things? prudent, and he shall know them? for the ways of the LORD are right, and the just shall walk in them: but the transgressors shall fall therein.

Notes

1 14:2 receive…: or, give good

2 14:5 grow: or, blossom

3 14:5 cast…: Heb. strike

4 14:6 spread: Heb. go

5 14:7 grow: or, blossom

6 14:7 scent: or, memorial
