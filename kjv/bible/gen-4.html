Cain and Abel

(Hebrews 11:4)

1And Adam knew Eve his wife; and she conceived, and bare Cain1, and said, I have gotten a man from the LORD. 2And she again bare his brother Abel2. And Abel was a keeper3 of sheep, but Cain was a tiller of the ground.

3And in process4 of time it came to pass, that Cain brought of the fruit of the ground an offering unto the LORD. 4And Abel, he also brought of the firstlings of his flock5 and of the fat thereof. And the LORD had respect unto Abel and to his offering: 5But unto Cain and to his offering he had not respect. And Cain was very wroth, and his countenance fell.

6And the LORD said unto Cain, Why art thou wroth? and why is thy countenance fallen? 7If thou doest well, shalt thou not be accepted6? and if thou doest not well, sin lieth at the door. And unto7 thee shall be his desire, and thou shalt rule over him. 8And Cain talked with Abel his brother: and it came to pass, when they were in the field, that Cain rose up against Abel his brother, and slew him.

9And the LORD said unto Cain, Where is Abel thy brother? And he said, I know not: Am I my brother’s keeper? 10And he said, What hast thou done? the voice of thy brother’s blood8 crieth unto me from the ground. 11And now art thou cursed from the earth, which hath opened her mouth to receive thy brother’s blood from thy hand; 12When thou tillest the ground, it shall not henceforth yield unto thee her strength; a fugitive and a vagabond shalt thou be in the earth.

13And Cain said unto the LORD, My9 punishment is greater than I can bear. 14Behold, thou hast driven me out this day from the face of the earth; and from thy face shall I be hid; and I shall be a fugitive and a vagabond in the earth; and it shall come to pass, that every one that findeth me shall slay me. 15And the LORD said unto him, Therefore whosoever slayeth Cain, vengeance shall be taken on him sevenfold. And the LORD set a mark upon Cain, lest any finding him should kill him.

16And Cain went out from the presence of the LORD, and dwelt in the land of Nod, on the east of Eden.

The Descendants of Cain

17And Cain knew his wife; and she conceived, and bare Enoch10: and he builded a city, and called the name of the city, after the name of his son, Enoch. 18And unto Enoch was born Irad: and Irad begat Mehujael: and Mehujael begat Methusael: and Methusael begat Lamech11.

19And Lamech took unto him two wives: the name of the one was Adah, and the name of the other Zillah. 20And Adah bare Jabal: he was the father of such as dwell in tents, and of such as have cattle. 21And his brother’s name was Jubal: he was the father of all such as handle the harp and organ. 22And Zillah, she also bare Tubal-cain, an instructer12 of every artificer in brass and iron: and the sister of Tubal-cain was Naamah.

23And Lamech said unto his wives, Adah and Zillah, Hear my voice; ye wives of Lamech, hearken unto my speech: for I have13 slain a man to my wounding, and a young man to my hurt14. 24If Cain shall be avenged sevenfold, truly Lamech seventy and sevenfold.

Seth and Enosh

25And Adam knew his wife again; and she bare a son, and called his name Seth15: For God, said she, hath appointed me another seed instead of Abel, whom Cain slew. 26And to Seth, to him also there was born a son; and he called his name Enos16: then began men to call17 upon the name of the LORD.

Notes

1 4:1 Cain: that is, Gotten, or, Acquired

2 4:2 Abel: Heb. Hebel

3 4:2 a keeper: Heb. a feeder

4 4:3 in process…: Heb. at the end of days

5 4:4 flock: Heb. sheep, or, goats

6 4:7 be accepted: or, have the excellency

7 4:7 unto…: or, subject unto thee

8 4:10 blood: Heb. bloods

9 4:13 My…: or, Mine iniquity is greater than that it may be forgiven

10 4:17 Enoch: Heb. Chanoch

11 4:18 Lamech: Heb. Lemech

12 4:22 instructer: Heb. whetter

13 4:23 I have…: or, I would slay a man in my wound, etc.

14 4:23 to my hurt: or, in my hurt

15 4:25 Seth: Heb. Sheth: that is Appointed, or, Put

16 4:26 Enos: Heb. Enosh

17 4:26 to call…: or, to call themselves by the name of the Lord
