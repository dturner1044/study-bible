Zophar: Destruction Awaits the Wicked

1Then answered Zophar the Naamathite, and said, 2Therefore do my thoughts cause me to answer, and for this I make1 haste. 3I have heard the check of my reproach, and the spirit of my understanding causeth me to answer. 4Knowest thou not this of old, since man was placed upon earth, 5That the triumphing of the wicked is short2, and the joy of the hypocrite but for a moment? 6Though his excellency mount up to the heavens, and his head reach unto the clouds3; 7Yet he shall perish for ever like his own dung: they which have seen him shall say, Where is he? 8He shall fly away as a dream, and shall not be found: yea, he shall be chased away as a vision of the night. 9The eye also which saw him shall see him no more; neither shall his place any more behold him.

10His children4 shall seek to please the poor, and his hands shall restore their goods. 11His bones are full of the sin of his youth, which shall lie down with him in the dust. 12Though wickedness be sweet in his mouth, though he hide it under his tongue; 13Though he spare it, and forsake it not; but keep it still within5 his mouth: 14Yet his meat in his bowels is turned, it is the gall of asps within him. 15He hath swallowed down riches, and he shall vomit them up again: God shall cast them out of his belly. 16He shall suck the poison of asps: the viper’s tongue shall slay him. 17He shall not see the rivers, the floods6, the brooks of honey and butter. 18That which he laboured for shall he restore, and shall not swallow it down: according to his7 substance shall the restitution be, and he shall not rejoice therein. 19Because he hath oppressed8 and hath forsaken the poor; because he hath violently taken away an house which he builded not; 20Surely he shall not feel9 quietness in his belly, he shall not save of that which he desired. 21There shall none10 of his meat be left; therefore shall no man look for his goods. 22In the fulness of his sufficiency he shall be in straits: every hand of the wicked11 shall come upon him.

23When he is about to fill his belly, God shall cast the fury of his wrath upon him, and shall rain it upon him while he is eating. 24He shall flee from the iron weapon, and the bow of steel shall strike him through. 25It is drawn, and cometh out of the body; yea, the glittering sword cometh out of his gall: terrors are upon him. 26All darkness shall be hid in his secret places: a fire not blown shall consume him; it shall go ill with him that is left in his tabernacle. 27The heaven shall reveal his iniquity; and the earth shall rise up against him. 28The increase of his house shall depart, and his goods shall flow away in the day of his wrath. 29This is the portion of a wicked man from God, and the heritage appointed12 unto him by God.

Notes

1 20:2 I make…: Heb. my haste is in me

2 20:5 short: Heb. from near

3 20:6 clouds: Heb. cloud

4 20:10 His children…: or, The poor shall oppress his children

5 20:13 within…: Heb. in the midst of his palate

6 20:17 the floods…: or, streaming brooks

7 20:18 his…: Heb. the substance of his exchange

8 20:19 oppressed: Heb. crushed

9 20:20 feel: Heb. know

10 20:21 none…: or, be none left for his meat

11 20:22 wicked: or, troublesome

12 20:29 appointed…: Heb. of his decree from God
