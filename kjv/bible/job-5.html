Eliphaz Continues: God Blesses those Who Seek Him

1Call now, if there be any that will answer thee; and to which of the saints wilt thou turn1? 2For wrath killeth the foolish man, and envy2 slayeth the silly one. 3I have seen the foolish taking root: but suddenly I cursed his habitation. 4His children are far from safety, and they are crushed in the gate, neither is there any to deliver them. 5Whose harvest the hungry eateth up, and taketh it even out of the thorns, and the robber swalloweth up their substance.

6Although affliction3 cometh not forth of the dust, neither doth trouble spring out of the ground; 7Yet man is born unto trouble4, as the sparks5 fly upward. 8I would seek unto God, and unto God would I commit my cause: 9Which doeth great things and unsearchable6; marvellous things without7 number: 10Who giveth rain upon the earth, and sendeth waters upon the fields8: 11To set up on high those that be low; that those which mourn may be exalted to safety. 12He disappointeth the devices of the crafty, so that their hands cannot perform their enterprise9. 13He taketh the wise in their own craftiness: and the counsel of the froward is carried headlong. 14They meet10 with darkness in the daytime, and grope in the noonday as in the night. 15But he saveth the poor from the sword, from their mouth, and from the hand of the mighty. 16So the poor hath hope, and iniquity stoppeth her mouth.

17Behold, happy is the man whom God correcteth: therefore despise not thou the chastening of the Almighty: 18For he maketh sore, and bindeth up: he woundeth, and his hands make whole. 19He shall deliver thee in six troubles: yea, in seven there shall no evil touch thee. 20In famine he shall redeem thee from death: and in war from the power11 of the sword. 21Thou shalt be hid from12 the scourge of the tongue: neither shalt thou be afraid of destruction when it cometh. 22At destruction and famine thou shalt laugh: neither shalt thou be afraid of the beasts of the earth. 23For thou shalt be in league with the stones of the field: and the beasts of the field shall be at peace with thee. 24And thou shalt know that thy tabernacle13 shall be in peace; and thou shalt visit thy habitation, and shalt not sin14. 25Thou shalt know also that thy seed shall be great15, and thine offspring as the grass of the earth. 26Thou shalt come to thy grave in a full age, like as a shock of corn cometh in16 in his season. 27Lo this, we have searched it, so it is; hear it, and know thou it for17 thy good.

Notes

1 5:1 turn: or, look?

2 5:2 envy: or, indignation

3 5:6 affliction: or, iniquity

4 5:7 trouble: or, labour

6 5:9 unsearchable: Heb. there is no search

7 5:9 without…: Heb. till there be no number

8 5:10 fields: Heb. outplaces

9 5:12 their enterprise: or, any thing

10 5:14 meet…: or, run into

11 5:20 power: Heb. hands

12 5:21 from…: or, when the tongue scourgeth

13 5:24 thy tabernacle…: or, peace is thy tabernacle

14 5:24 sin: or, err

15 5:25 great: or, much

16 5:26 cometh in: Heb. ascendeth

17 5:27 for…: Heb. for thyself
