Eliphaz: Can a Man Be of Use to God?

1Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said, 2Can a man be profitable unto God, as he1 that is wise may be profitable unto himself? 3Is it any pleasure to the Almighty, that thou art righteous? or is it gain to him, that thou makest thy ways perfect? 4Will he reprove thee for fear of thee? will he enter with thee into judgment?

5Is not thy wickedness great? and thine iniquities infinite? 6For thou hast taken a pledge from thy brother for nought, and stripped the naked2 of their clothing. 7Thou hast not given water to the weary to drink, and thou hast withholden bread from the hungry. 8But as for the mighty3 man, he had the earth; and the honourable4 man dwelt in it. 9Thou hast sent widows away empty, and the arms of the fatherless have been broken. 10Therefore snares are round about thee, and sudden fear troubleth thee; 11Or darkness, that thou canst not see; and abundance of waters cover thee. 12Is not God in the height of heaven? and behold the height of the stars5, how high they are! 13And thou sayest, How6 doth God know? can he judge through the dark cloud? 14Thick clouds are a covering to him, that he seeth not; and he walketh in the circuit of heaven.

15Hast thou marked the old way which wicked men have trodden? 16Which were cut down out of time, whose7 foundation was overflown with a flood: 17Which said unto God, Depart from us: and what can the Almighty do for8 them? 18Yet he filled their houses with good things: but the counsel of the wicked is far from me. 19The righteous see it, and are glad: and the innocent laugh them to scorn. 20Whereas our substance9 is not cut down, but the remnant10 of them the fire consumeth.

21Acquaint now thyself with him11, and be at peace: thereby good shall come unto thee. 22Receive, I pray thee, the law from his mouth, and lay up his words in thine heart. 23If thou return to the Almighty, thou shalt be built up, thou shalt put away iniquity far from thy tabernacles. 24Then shalt thou lay up gold as dust12, and the gold of Ophir as the stones of the brooks. 25Yea, the Almighty shall be thy defence13, and thou shalt have plenty14 of silver. 26For then shalt thou have thy delight in the Almighty, and shalt lift up thy face unto God. 27Thou shalt make thy prayer unto him, and he shall hear thee, and thou shalt pay thy vows. 28Thou shalt also decree a thing, and it shall be established unto thee: and the light shall shine upon thy ways. 29When men are cast down, then thou shalt say, There is lifting up; and he shall save the humble15 person. 30He shall16 deliver the island of the innocent: and it is delivered by the pureness of thine hands.

Notes

1 22:2 as he…: or, if he may be profitable, doth his good success depend thereon?

2 22:6 the naked…: Heb. the clothes of the naked

3 22:8 mighty…: Heb. man of arm

4 22:8 honourable…: Heb. eminent, or, accepted for countenance

5 22:12 height of the stars: Heb. head of the stars

6 22:13 How: or, What

7 22:16 whose…: Heb. a flood was poured upon their foundation

8 22:17 for: or, to

9 22:20 substance: or, estate

10 22:20 the remnant…: or, their excellency

11 22:21 him: that is, God

12 22:24 as dust: or, on the dust

13 22:25 defence: or, gold

14 22:25 plenty…: Heb. silver of strength

15 22:29 the humble…: Heb. him that hath low eyes

16 22:30 He shall…: or, The innocent shall deliver the island
