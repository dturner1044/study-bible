The Overthrow of Nineveh

1He1 that dasheth in pieces is come up before thy face: keep the munition, watch the way, make thy loins strong, fortify thy power mightily. 2For the LORD hath turned away the excellency of Jacob2, as the excellency of Israel: for the emptiers have emptied them out, and marred their vine branches. 3The shield of his mighty men is made red, the valiant men are in scarlet3: the chariots shall be with flaming4 torches in the day of his preparation, and the fir trees shall be terribly shaken. 4The chariots shall rage in the streets, they5 shall justle one against another in the broad ways: they shall seem like torches, they shall run like the lightnings. 5He shall recount his worthies6: they shall stumble in their walk; they shall make haste to the wall thereof, and the defence7 shall be prepared. 6The gates of the rivers shall be opened, and the palace shall be dissolved8. 7And Huzzab9 shall be led10 away captive, she shall be brought up, and her maids shall lead her as with the voice of doves, tabering upon their breasts. 8But Nineveh is of old11 like a pool of water: yet they shall flee away. Stand, stand, shall they cry; but none shall look back12. 9Take ye the spoil of silver, take the spoil of gold: for13 there is none end of the store and glory out of all the pleasant14 furniture. 10She is empty, and void, and waste: and the heart melteth, and the knees smite together, and much pain is in all loins, and the faces of them all gather blackness.

11Where is the dwelling of the lions, and the feedingplace of the young lions, where the lion, even the old lion, walked, and the lion’s whelp, and none made them afraid? 12The lion did tear in pieces enough for his whelps, and strangled for his lionesses, and filled his holes with prey, and his dens with ravin. 13Behold, I am against thee, saith the LORD of hosts, and I will burn her chariots in the smoke, and the sword shall devour thy young lions: and I will cut off thy prey from the earth, and the voice of thy messengers shall no more be heard.

Notes

1 2:1 He…: or, The disperser, or, hammer

2 2:2 the excellency of Jacob…: or, the pride of Jacob as the pride, etc

3 2:3 in scarlet: or, dyed scarlet

4 2:3 flaming: or, fiery

5 2:4 they: Heb. their show

6 2:5 worthies: or, gallants

7 2:5 defence: Heb. covering, or, coverer

8 2:6 dissolved: or, molten

9 2:7 Huzzab: or, that which was established, or, there was a stand made

10 2:7 led…: or, discovered

11 2:8 of old: or, from the days that she hath been

12 2:8 look back: or, cause them to turn

13 2:9 for…: or, and their infinite store, etc

14 2:9 pleasant…: Heb. vessels of desire
