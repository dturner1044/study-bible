Judgment to Come

(Isaiah 7:17–25)

1The word of the LORD that came to Micah the Morasthite in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, which he saw concerning Samaria and Jerusalem. 2Hear, all ye1 people; hearken, O earth, and all that2 therein is: and let the Lord GOD be witness against you, the Lord from his holy temple. 3For, behold, the LORD cometh forth out of his place, and will come down, and tread upon the high places of the earth. 4And the mountains shall be molten under him, and the valleys shall be cleft, as wax before the fire, and as the waters that are poured down a steep3 place. 5For the transgression of Jacob is all this, and for the sins of the house of Israel. What is the transgression of Jacob? is it not Samaria? and what are the high places of Judah? are they not Jerusalem? 6Therefore I will make Samaria as an heap of the field, and as plantings of a vineyard: and I will pour down the stones thereof into the valley, and I will discover the foundations thereof. 7And all the graven images thereof shall be beaten to pieces, and all the hires thereof shall be burned with the fire, and all the idols thereof will I lay desolate: for she gathered it of the hire of an harlot, and they shall return to the hire of an harlot.

Weeping and Mourning

8Therefore I will wail and howl, I will go stripped and naked: I will make a wailing like the dragons, and mourning as the owls4. 9For her5 wound is incurable; for it is come unto Judah; he is come unto the gate of my people, even to Jerusalem.

10Declare ye it not at Gath, weep ye not at all: in the house of Aphrah6 roll thyself in the dust. 11Pass ye away, thou7 inhabitant8 of Saphir, having thy shame naked: the inhabitant of Zaanan9 came not forth in the mourning of Beth-ezel10; he shall receive of you his standing. 12For the inhabitant11 of Maroth waited12 carefully for good: but evil came down from the LORD unto the gate of Jerusalem. 13O thou inhabitant13 of Lachish, bind the chariot to the swift beast: she is the beginning of the sin to the daughter of Zion: for the transgressions of Israel were found in thee. 14Therefore shalt thou give presents to14 Moresheth-gath: the houses of Achzib15 shall be a lie to the kings of Israel. 15Yet will I bring an heir unto thee, O inhabitant16 of Mareshah: he17 shall come unto Adullam the glory of Israel. 16Make thee bald, and poll thee for thy delicate children; enlarge thy baldness as the eagle; for they are gone into captivity from thee.

Notes

1 1:2 all ye…: Heb. ye people, all of them

2 1:2 all that…: Heb. the fulness thereof

3 1:4 a steep…: Heb. a descent

4 1:8 owls: Heb. daughters of the owl

5 1:9 her…: or, she is grievously sick of her wounds

6 1:10 Aphrah: that is, Dust

7 1:11 thou…: or, thou that dwellest fairly

8 1:11 inhabitant: Heb. inhabitress

9 1:11 Zaanan: or, The country of flocks

10 1:11 Beth-ezel: or, A place near

11 1:12 inhabitant: Heb. inhabitress

12 1:12 waited…: or, was grieved

13 1:13 inhabitant: Heb. inhabitress

14 1:14 to…: or, for Moresheth-gath

15 1:14 Achzib: that is, A lie

16 1:15 inhabitant: Heb. inhabitress

17 1:15 he…: or, the glory of Israel shall, etc
