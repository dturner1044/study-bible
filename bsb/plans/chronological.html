Appendix B

The Bible in a year, in order of events

This plan reads the whole Bible once in a year in the order in which its events took place, as nearly as whole chapters allow. Job stands among the patriarchs; psalms whose titles name an occasion are read at that occasion and the rest with the period of their collection; the prophets are read beside the reigns they addressed; the four Gospels are interleaved; and the letters fall into the narrative of Acts. Where the date of a book is disputed a conventional placing has been followed. Days are of roughly equal length (about 85 verses).
