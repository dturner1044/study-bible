Appendix A

M‘Cheyne’s calendar for reading the Bible in a year

Robert Murray M‘Cheyne drew up this calendar for his congregation at St Peter’s, Dundee, at the end of 1842. It gives four portions a day: two for family worship and two for private (“secret”) reading. Followed for a year it goes once through the Old Testament and twice through the New Testament and the Psalms. The readings below follow his own calendar, including its divided chapters; he set none for 29 February.
