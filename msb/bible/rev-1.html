Prologue

(Daniel 12:1–13)

1This is the revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave Him to show His servants what must soon1 come to pass. He made it known by sending His angel to His servant John, 2who testifies to everything he saw.2 This is the word of God and the testimony of Jesus Christ.

3Blessed is the one who reads aloud3 the words of this prophecy, and blessed are those who hear and obey what is written in it, because the time is near.

John Greets the Seven Churches

4John,

To the seven churches in the province of Asia:4

Grace and peace to you from God who5 is and was and is to come, and from the seven spirits6 before His throne, 5and from Jesus Christ, the faithful witness, the firstborn from the dead, and the ruler of the kings of the earth.

To Him who loves us and has washed7 us from our sins by His blood, 6who has made us to be a kingdom, priests to His God and Father—to Him be the glory and power forever and ever!8 Amen.

7Behold, He is coming with the clouds, and every eye will see Him—even those who pierced Him. And all the tribes of the earth will mourn because of Him. So shall it be! Amen.

8“I am the Alpha and the Omega,9” says the Lord God, who is and was and is to come—the Almighty.

John’s Vision on Patmos

9I, John, your brother and partner in the tribulation and kingdom and perseverance that are in Jesus, was on the island of Patmos because of the word of God and my testimony about Jesus Christ.1010On the Lord’s day I was in the Spirit, and I heard behind me a loud voice like a trumpet, 11saying,11 “Write on a scroll what you see and send it to the seven churches:12 to Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia, and Laodicea.”

12And there I turned13 to see the voice that was speaking with me. And having turned, I saw seven golden lampstands, 13and among the seven14 lampstands was One like the Son of Man,15 dressed in a long robe, with a golden sash around His chest. 14The hair of His head was white like wool, as white as snow, and His eyes were like a blazing fire. 15His feet were like polished bronze refined in a furnace, and His voice was like the roar of many waters. 16He held in His right hand seven stars, and a sharp double-edged sword came from His mouth. His face was like the sun shining at its brightest.

17When I saw Him, I fell at His feet like a dead man. But He placed His right hand on me and said,16 “Do not be afraid. I am the First and the Last, 18the Living One. I was dead, and behold, now I am alive forever and ever. Amen.17 And I hold the keys of Death and of Hades.18

19Therefore19 write down the things you have seen, the things that are, and the things that will happen after this. 20This is the mystery of the seven stars you saw in My right hand and of the seven golden lampstands:20 The seven stars are the angels of the seven churches, and the seven lampstands are the seven churches.
