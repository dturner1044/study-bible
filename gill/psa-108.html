Introduction to Psalm 108

"A Song [or] Psalm of David".

This psalm consists of several passages out of the fifty seventh and sixtieth psalms, with very little variation. Jarchi and Kimchi refer it to the times of the Messiah. The title in the Syriac version is,

concerning the calling of the Gentiles,

to which, no doubt, it has respect.

Psalm 108:1

O God, my heart is fixed; I will sing and give praise, even with my glory.

A song. A Psalm of David. My heart is steadfast, O God; I will sing and make music with all my being.

My heart is steadfast, O God; I will sing and make music with all my being.

O God, my heart is fixed; I will sing and give praise, &c. From hence to Ps 108:6 the words are taken out of Ps 57:7-11, which see.

Even with my glory; my tongue; in Ps 57:8, it is read, "awake up my glory". see Gill on Ps 57:7,

Psalm 108:4

For thy mercy is great above the heavens: and thy truth reacheth unto the clouds.

For Your loving devotion extends beyond the heavens, and Your faithfulness reaches to the clouds.

For Your loving devotion extends beyond the heavens, and Your faithfulness reaches to the clouds.

For thy mercy is great above the heavens, &c.]. It is in Ps 57:10, "thy mercy is great unto the heavens". see Gill on Ps 57:10.

Psalm 108:6

That thy beloved may be delivered: save with thy right hand, and answer me.

Respond and save us with Your right hand, that Your beloved may be delivered.

Respond and save us with Your right hand, that Your beloved may be delivered.

That thy beloved may be delivered, &c. From hence to the end of the psalm the words are taken out of Ps 60:5-12. see Gill on Ps 60:5.

Psalm 108:9

Moab is my washpot; over Edom will I cast out my shoe; over Philistia will I triumph.

Moab is My washbasin; upon Edom I toss My sandal; over Philistia I shout in triumph.”

Moab is My washbasin; upon Edom I toss My sandal; over Philistia I shout in triumph.”

Over Philistia will I triumph, &c. In Ps 60:8, it is, "Philistia, triumph thou because of me"; see Gill on Ps 60:8.

Psalm 108:11

Wilt not thou, O God, who hast cast us off? and wilt not thou, O God, go forth with our hosts?

Have You not rejected us, O God? Will You no longer march out, O God, with our armies?

Have You not rejected us, O God? Will You no longer march out, O God, with our armies?

And wilt not thou, O God, go forth with our hosts? &c. In Ps 60:10, it is, "and thou, O God, which didst not go out with our armies". see Gill on Ps 60:10.
